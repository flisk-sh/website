Hire one a week — or unblock all five at once

The textbook advice is onboarding developers one per week so each gets attention. Reality hires the whole team on one Monday. Flisk makes the realistic way work.

The +1 developer per week ideal

Staggered starts mean every newcomer gets a mentor, a working setup guide and review time. It takes five weeks to staff the team — and assumes someone senior has five free weeks to babysit environments.

The 5-at-once reality, unblocked

Hire all five together. Nobody needs a mentor for setup because there is no setup: the manifest generates every config, the doctor verifies every machine. Senior time stays on architecture, not on .env files.
